Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy explains how users may use PeakCut. Its purpose is to protect users, third parties, connected platforms and the security and reliability of the service.

2. Lawful Use

You may use PeakCut only for lawful purposes and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

You must not use PeakCut in a way that violates the rights of another person or organization.

3. Copyright and Intellectual Property

You must only upload, process, edit or distribute videos, audio, images, music and other materials when you own the necessary rights or have permission to use them.

You must not use PeakCut to knowingly infringe copyrights, trademarks, licenses or other intellectual property rights.

4. Prohibited Content

You must not use PeakCut to create, process, store or distribute content that is illegal or that violates the rights of others.

This includes content involving exploitation, threats, harassment, unauthorized private information or other unlawful material.

5. Privacy and Personal Data

You must respect the privacy and data protection rights of other people.

Do not upload or process personal data, recordings, images or videos of other people unless you have a lawful basis and any required permissions.

6. Connected Platforms

When using integrations with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or other platforms, you must follow the applicable platform terms, community guidelines, developer policies and API requirements.

You must not use PeakCut to bypass platform restrictions, manipulate engagement metrics or perform unauthorized actions.

7. Accounts and Authentication

You must keep your login credentials, access tokens, API keys and other authentication information secure.

You must not access accounts belonging to other people without permission or attempt to bypass authentication or access controls.

8. Security and System Abuse

You must not:

  • Attempt to access systems or data without authorization.
  • Disrupt or overload PeakCut or its infrastructure.
  • Introduce malware, malicious code or harmful files.
  • Attempt to bypass security mechanisms or usage restrictions.
  • Reverse engineer or interfere with the service in an unlawful manner.

9. AI Features

You must use AI-powered features responsibly and review generated results before publishing or distributing them.

You must not use AI features to create unlawful, deceptive, abusive or rights-infringing content.

10. Enforcement

If we have a reasonable basis to believe that this policy has been violated, we may investigate the issue and take appropriate action.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include restricting features, suspending access or terminating an account where permitted by applicable law.

11. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of misuse of PeakCut or a possible violation of this policy, you may contact us at: streamerclips38@outlook.de

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.
