Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PeakCut handles information when you use the application.

2. Information We Process

PeakCut may process video files selected by users, account connection information and basic profile information provided by connected platforms.

When you connect TikTok, PeakCut requests basic profile information, such as your TikTok user ID, display name and profile picture, through the user.info.basic permission.

3. Video Uploads

When you choose to upload a video to TikTok, PeakCut uses the TikTok Content Posting API to transfer the video as a draft. You can review and publish the draft through TikTok.

4. Use of Information

Information is processed to provide video clipping, account connection and content upload functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

When you connect third-party platforms such as TikTok or Instagram, their own privacy policies and terms also apply.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through PeakCut. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
