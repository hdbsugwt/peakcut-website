Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PeakCut processes personal data when you use the PeakCut service. The data controller is enessaurusrex der sklave vom osmanischen reich ("we", "us").

2. Information We Process

  • Account information, including email address, name, password hash, Google account ID and profile picture where applicable.
  • Video URLs, uploaded videos, downloaded media, transcripts, word timestamps, clips, titles, captions and descriptions.
  • Channel and creator information, including publicly available information from supported platforms.
  • Connected account information and access tokens for YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
  • AI usage records, including request times, purposes and token usage.
  • Technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, session records, error logs and feedback.
  • Optional integration settings, including Discord webhook URLs and Telegram bot tokens and chat IDs.

3. Video Processing and Transcription

Videos and audio are processed to identify moments, generate clips and create captions. Transcripts and related processing files may be stored with the relevant job.

Speech recognition normally runs locally on the machine or server running PeakCut. If an external speech-recognition server is configured, audio segments may be transmitted to that server for processing.

4. AI Processing

Depending on the features used and the configured AI provider, PeakCut may send transcripts, timestamps, video titles, channel information, loudness measurements, chat activity figures and text to an AI provider.
