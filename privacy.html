Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PeakCut processes personal data when you use the PeakCut service. The data controller is enessaurusrex der sklave vom osmanischen reich ("we", "us").

2. Information We Process

  • Account information, including email address, name, password hash, Google account ID and profile picture where applicable.
  • Video URLs, uploaded videos, downloaded media, transcripts, word timestamps, clips, titles, captions and descriptions.
  • Channel and creator information, including publicly available information from supported platforms.
  • Connected account information and access tokens for YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
  • AI usage records, including request times, purposes and token usage.
  • Technical information such as IP addresses, browser information, session records, error logs and feedback.
  • Optional integration settings, including Discord webhook URLs and Telegram bot tokens and chat IDs.

3. Video Processing and Transcription

Videos and audio are processed to identify moments, generate clips and create captions. Transcripts and related processing files may be stored with the relevant job.

Speech recognition normally runs locally on the machine or server running PeakCut. If an external speech-recognition server is configured, audio segments may be transmitted to that server for processing.

4. AI Processing

Depending on the features used and the configured AI provider, PeakCut may send transcripts, timestamps, video titles, channel information, loudness measurements, chat activity figures and text to an AI provider.

If a webcam or facecam analysis feature is used, selected image frames may also be sent to the configured AI provider for analysis.

PeakCut supports different AI providers. The provider used depends on the configuration and features selected by the user. Each provider processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Social Media Connections

PeakCut can connect to supported social media platforms, including YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

When a user connects an account, PeakCut may process account identifiers, display names, channel information and authentication tokens required to provide the requested features.

These tokens are used to authenticate requests and perform actions authorized by the user. Users can disconnect their accounts through the application.

When content is uploaded to a connected platform, the platform processes that content according to its own privacy policy and terms.

6. Google User Data

When Google services are connected, PeakCut may process Google account identifiers, profile information, YouTube channel information and OAuth authentication tokens.

Google user data is used only to provide the features requested by the user, such as accessing channel information or uploading content to YouTube.

PeakCut does not sell Google user data. Google user data is not used for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Use of Google services is also subject to Google's Privacy Policy: Google Privacy Policy.

7. Emails and Notifications

PeakCut may send account-related emails, such as email verification messages and password reset links, using a configured email service.

If optional Discord or Telegram notifications are enabled, relevant notification information may be transmitted to the configured service.

8. Cookies and Sessions

PeakCut uses session-related information to maintain user login status and protect authenticated application functions.

Session cookies or similar technical mechanisms may be used to keep users signed in.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Personal data and application files may be stored on the machine or server running PeakCut, including databases, configuration files, uploaded media, generated clips, transcripts and temporary processing files.

Data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain account functionality, comply with applicable legal obligations or resolve disputes.

Users may delete available jobs, clips or account information through the application where these functions are provided.
